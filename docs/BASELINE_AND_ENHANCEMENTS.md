# SubTrack — baseline first, evidence-led enhancements

## What cannot be traded away

The two supplied attachments are complementary, not competing briefs:

- **Baseline A: Brightloop’s four-page Subscription Management System statement.** Cover all **28** tracked requirements: AUTH-01–05, CAT-01–02, PLAN-01–03, QUOTE-01, SUB-01–04, BILL-01–05, DISC-01–04, PAY-01–02 and FIN-01–02. [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md) maps each to observable evidence. These IDs are our labels, not organizer numbering.
- **Baseline B: five must-haves.** Persisted dynamic data; responsive, consistent UI; robust validation; intuitive navigation/spacing; genuine Git participation across the team. Its **four nice-to-haves** are backend/API/data-model/local-database competence; understanding and adapting AI/snippets; local/offline resilience; and fashionable technology only where useful. See [HACKATHON_CONSTRAINTS.md](HACKATHON_CONSTRAINTS.md).

[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) resolves implementation ambiguities with **proposed team policies**, not additional organizer rules. Do not present our tax/discount ordering, period boundaries, staff permissions or renewal policy as quoted requirements. If organizers clarify a conflict, update the decision and affected tests together.

“Overfit” means making every stated requirement easy to verify—not guessing secret scoring weights, inventing official bonus points or hiding broken paths behind a polished demo. The roadmap remains the execution order; this list is an enhancement queue, not permission to expand scope indefinitely.

**Scope cap:** this is a menu, not a backlog to finish in full. Prefer one or two low-risk improvements embedded in required screens. A test report or simple table is enough; do not build separate inspector/panel applications. Product notes and the final Closed subscription state are mandatory, not enhancements.

## Release gate

Before selecting extras, demonstrate every baseline requirement with real persisted data, negative tests and honest evidence gaps. Complete [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)'s core gates first. Each retained enhancement needs one reproducible walkthrough, permission checks, loading/empty/error states and a named owner who understands it. Rehearse the short [JUDGE_WALKTHROUGH.md](JUDGE_WALKTHROUGH.md); keep deeper proof available on demand rather than lengthening every demo. A working, explainable core beats seven unfinished extras.
